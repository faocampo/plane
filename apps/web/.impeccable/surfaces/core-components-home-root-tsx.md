# Curve Home surface brief

- Scope and mode: Curve-enabled workspace Home in Operate mode.
- Audience: product owners, delivery leads, engineers, approvers, and operators returning to a workspace.
- Job: identify the cross-project decision, exception, or handoff that deserves attention before resuming detailed work.
- Primary actions: open a filter-preserving Initiative queue, Inbox, or My work view.
- Content: high-risk active Initiatives, Initiatives in alignment, unread mentions or updates, compact assigned-work health, and user-configured recent context tools.
- Constraint: Home summarizes and routes; My work remains the record-level task-management surface, and Inbox remains the complete notification history.
- Truth behavior: counts cover every server cursor page; failed or unresolved evidence is labeled unavailable rather than rendered as zero.
- Direction: a calm control-room queue paired with a compact work pulse, followed by optional recent context, quick links, and stickies.
- Memorable interaction: every attention row is a direct route into the authoritative destination rather than a second embedded task list.
- Unresolved dependencies: approval counts require GateDecision delivery; derived Delivery projects require Roadmap Item work bindings.
